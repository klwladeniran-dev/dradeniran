What we can screen for

Four checks. One easy step.

Ages 25–69 · every 5 yrs

Colorectal

Ages 50–74

Ages 55–80 · 20+ yrs smoking

Eligibility depends on your age, history and risk — we’ll sort out what’s right for you.

Source: Cancer Care Ontario — cancercareontario.ca

Why it works · colorectal example

Found early, the odds are far better.

▲0%5-year survival when caught early (stage 1)
▼0%5-year survival when caught late (stage 4)

Screening finds colorectal cancer before you feel it — and can even catch and remove pre-cancers (polyps) before they ever become cancer. The earlier it’s found, the better the outcome.

Source: Canadian Cancer Society · survival by stage — cancer.ca
Caught early, 5-year survival is about 92% — versus roughly 11% once it has spread.
