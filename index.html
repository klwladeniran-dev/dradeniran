What I offer

Full primary care for people of all ages in Windsor and the surrounding area.

🩺

Family Medicine

I give full, regular care for the whole family — babies, children, adults, and seniors. I take the time to learn your health story, not just treat the problem of the day. Good family medicine is built on trust over many years, and I am here for the long run.

📋

Annual Physical Exams

A yearly check-up is one of the best things you can do for your health. I do a full head-to-toe exam, order blood tests, and give health tips based on your age, lifestyle, and risk factors. Catching problems early makes a big difference.

🫀

Chronic Disease Management

Living with a long-term health condition is manageable with the right support. I provide ongoing care for:

  • Diabetes
  • High blood pressure (hypertension)
  • Asthma and COPD
  • Heart disease
  • Arthritis
  • Thyroid conditions
  • Chronic kidney disease
  • Obesity and weight management
  • Other long-term health conditions

I work with you to manage medications, track your progress, and update your care plan as your needs change.

🧠

Mental Health Support

Mental health is just as important as physical health. I provide care for anxiety, depression, stress, and other mental health concerns. I listen carefully and work with you to find a plan that fits your needs — whether that means counselling, medication, community support, or a referral to a specialist.

👶

Pediatric Care

From your baby's very first visit to their teen years, I am here to help your child grow up healthy.

  • Well-baby visits and growth check-ups
  • Childhood vaccines and immunization schedules
  • Illness visits and treatment
  • School, daycare, camp, and sports forms

Seniors Care

Older adults often have complex health needs. I take the time to look at the full picture.

Women's Health

Why it matters

The difference a family doctor makes

Having one doctor who knows you over time isn’t just more comfortable — the evidence links it to real, measurable benefits.

🤝

Someone who knows your whole story

Care from one doctor you see over time — not a different stranger at every visit. The relationship between you and your family doctor is one of the founding principles of family medicine, and the trust built over the years leads to more personal, better-informed decisions about your health.

SourceCollege of Family Physicians of Canada

❤️

Linked to a longer life

Seeing the same doctor over time is linked to living longer. A landmark review of 22 studies across 9 different countries found that, in the large majority, greater continuity of care went hand in hand with significantly lower death rates.

SourcePereira Gray et al., BMJ Open, 2018

🏥

Fewer ER visits and hospital stays

Patients who consistently see their own family doctor end up in the emergency department, and admitted to hospital, less often. In a large Canadian study this held true for patients at every level of health complexity.

SourceAnnals of Family Medicine, 2024 (Alberta)

🛡️

Problems caught early

A family doctor keeps your check-ups, vaccines and cancer screening on track, and is well placed to spot issues before they become serious. Continuity of care is consistently linked to a higher uptake of preventive care.

SourceCollege of Family Physicians of Canada · CMAJ

📊

Ongoing conditions, better controlled

For things like high blood pressure, diabetes, asthma and thyroid problems, an ongoing relationship is linked to better day-to-day control and outcomes — your doctor adjusts your care as your needs change.

SourceCMAJ, 2007 · PLOS One review, 2024

🧭

One place that coordinates everything

Your family doctor is your home base — making referrals, pulling together results from specialists and the hospital, reviewing your medications and making sure nothing slips through the cracks.

SourceCollege of Family Physicians of Canada

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

The things people most often want to know before booking a new family doctor in Windsor. Still unsure? Call (519) 800-8333.

Are you accepting new patients, and how do I register? top searched

Yes — I'm accepting new patients of all ages across Windsor and Essex County, often within the same week. To register, tap New Patient in the booking section above: it registers you and books your first visit through Ocean. Prefer to talk to someone? Call (519) 800-8333. You don't need a referral to join a family practice.

What should I bring to my first appointment? top searched

The most important thing is your valid OHIP card. If you've just applied and don't have the card yet, bring your temporary Ontario health registration certificate. It also helps to bring:

• A list of your current medications (or the bottles/blister packs)
• Brief notes on your past medical history, surgeries, and any specialists you see
• For children, their immunization (“yellow”) card

Arriving a few minutes early gives you time to complete the intake forms so we can spend the visit on your health.

I'm a newcomer or refugee — am I covered? top searched

You're very welcome here — caring for newcomers and refugees is a focus of this practice. I accept OHIP, Medavie Blue Cross, and the Interim Federal Health Program (IFHP). Doctor visits are fully covered under the IFHP for eligible refugees, claimants and protected persons, with no co-payment — just bring your IFHP certificate or refugee document to each visit. If your OHIP application is still being processed, come in and we'll help you get connected to care.

Do you accept walk-ins, or do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome, and booking ahead usually means a shorter wait and a time that suits you. Existing patients can book a follow-up, renewal or routine visit using the Existing Patient option above; new patients should start with New Patient. For anything urgent or life-threatening, please call 911 or go to your nearest emergency department.

Do I need a referral to see a family doctor?

No. You don't need a referral to register with or see a family doctor. A family physician is usually your first point of contact for most health concerns — and the one who arranges referrals to specialists when they're needed.

Can my whole family register, including my children?

Yes — this is a family practice caring for all ages, from newborns and children to teens, adults and seniors, so you can register the whole family. That includes well-baby and childhood checkups, immunizations, women's and seniors' health, chronic condition management, and preventive screening.

Please note that each person attending needs their own appointment booked, even when you come in together as a family — that way everyone gets their own dedicated time with me.

How do I book, and can I do it online?

Booking is online through Ocean. Use New Patient for your first visit or Existing Patient for a follow-up — both are in the booking section near the top of this page. Prefer to call? (519) 800-8333.

My current practice is based at Hope Health Central, 3955 Tecumseh Rd E, Windsor, Ontario. Clinic hours and a map are listed further up this page.
