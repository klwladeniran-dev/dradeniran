What I offer

Full primary care for people of all ages in Windsor and the surrounding area.

🩺

Family Medicine

I give full, regular care for the whole family — babies, children, adults, and seniors. I take the time to learn your health story, not just treat the problem of the day. Good family medicine is built on trust over many years, and I am here for the long run.

📋

Annual Physical Exams

A yearly check-up is one of the best things you can do for your health. I do a full head-to-toe exam, order blood tests, and give health tips based on your age, lifestyle, and risk factors. Catching problems early makes a big difference.

🫀

Chronic Disease Management

Living with a long-term health condition is manageable with the right support. I provide ongoing care for:

  • Diabetes
  • High blood pressure (hypertension)
  • Asthma and COPD
  • Heart disease
  • Arthritis
  • Thyroid conditions
  • Chronic kidney disease
  • Obesity and weight management
  • Other long-term health conditions

I work with you to manage medications, track your progress, and update your care plan as your needs change.

🧠

Mental Health Support

Mental health is just as important as physical health. I provide care for anxiety, depression, stress, and other mental health concerns. I listen carefully and work with you to find a plan that fits your needs — whether that means counselling, medication, community support, or a referral to a specialist.

👶

Pediatric Care

From your baby's very first visit to their teen years, I am here to help your child grow up healthy.

  • Well-baby visits and growth check-ups
  • Childhood vaccines and immunization schedules
  • Illness visits and treatment
  • School, daycare, camp, and sports forms
🏥

Seniors Care

Older adults often have complex health needs. I take the time to look at the full picture.

  • Full health assessments for older adults
  • Medication reviews — making sure all medications are safe together
  • Help with specialists and home care teams
  • Memory and thinking assessments
  • Fall prevention advice
🌸

Women's Health

I provide caring, private health services made for women.

  • Pap smear and cervical cancer screening coordination
  • Birth control advice and prescriptions
  • Prenatal care and specialist referrals
  • Hormone health and menopause support
  • Breast health screening coordination
💉

Immunizations & Vaccinations

Vaccines help keep you and your family safe from serious illness. I offer:

  • Routine vaccines for children and adults
  • Flu shots
  • Travel vaccines and travel health advice
  • COVID-19 and respiratory vaccines (when available)
🔬

Minor Procedures

I can safely remove ear wax buildup right here in the office — no hospital visit needed.

📝

Medical Forms & Documentation

I help with many types of health forms and reports.

  • Driver's Medical Exams (Ontario MTO — including commercial and renewal medicals)
  • WSIB forms and assessments
  • Disability and insurance forms
  • Sick notes and return-to-work forms
  • Pre-employment, school, daycare, and camp forms

Some forms not covered by OHIP may have a fee. Reception can share a fee schedule.

🔗

Specialist Referrals

When you need to see a specialist, I take care of the referral for you. I work with many specialists in Windsor and make sure they have all the health details they need. You will not have to figure out the system on your own.

I want every visit to feel easy and calm. Your questions will always be answered, and your care will be made just for you. If you are looking for a family doctor who will be a true partner in your health, I would be glad to welcome you.

I welcome all members of our community — including newcomers and refugees covered under Medavie Blue Cross / IFH.
